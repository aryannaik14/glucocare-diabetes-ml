Steps to Manage & Reverse Type 2 Diabetes

Type 2 diabetes is largely driven by lifestyle and diet. Here are foundational, proactive steps to help improve insulin sensitivity.

1. Dietary Adjustments

Limit carbohydrates and refined sugars to reduce blood glucose spikes. Focus on healthy fats, leafy greens, and moderate protein to stabilize insulin levels.

2. Intermittent Fasting

Give your digestive system a break to allow insulin levels to drop. Begin with a 12-hour overnight fasting window (e.g., 8 PM to 8 AM) and gradually increase.

3. Strength & Cardio

Skeletal muscle consumes glucose. Build muscle mass through resistance training and engage in daily cardiovascular activities, like brisk walking after meals.

4. Optimize Sleep & Stress

Poor sleep and high stress increase cortisol levels, which raises blood sugar. Aim for 7-8 hours of quality sleep and practice stress management techniques.
